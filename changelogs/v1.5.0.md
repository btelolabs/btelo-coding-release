### 2026-06-08

## What's New
- **Desktop app for Mac & Windows.** Btelo Coding now comes as a real desktop app you download from our website. One app with everything built in — just double-click to open and run. No setup.
- **Btelo Agent.** A new built-in AI agent you can pick right from the model switcher, next to Claude, Codex, Copilot and the rest. Run it with a local model or your own cloud key.
- **Connect without the terminal.** The iOS app now sends you straight to the desktop download — open the app, scan the QR, and you're paired. No commands to copy.
- **Browse every drive on Windows.** When your computer runs Windows, the file browser lets you hop between drives (C:, D:, E:…) with one tap.

## Improvements
- **Clearer downloads on the website.** Dedicated Mac and Windows buttons — including Windows on ARM — plus a quick look at the AI platforms we support: Claude, Codex, Copilot, DeepSeek, MiniMax and Btelo.
- **A model switcher that just works.** Your available AI agents always appear and stay in sync, each with its own logo.
- **Welcome video with sound.** The short intro that plays when you first open the desktop app now plays with audio.
- **Smoother iOS chat.** Cleaner message rendering, plus voice- and chat-input polish.

## Bug Fixes
- Folder names on Windows now show the folder, not the full path.
- The Btelo Agent settings now let you choose its model.
- Assorted stability and polish fixes.
