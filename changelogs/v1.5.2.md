### 2026-06-08

## What's New
- **Check for updates from Settings.** The General settings now show the version you're running and add a "Check for updates" button — click it to look for a newer release right away and update with one click. (Btelo Coding still checks on its own in the background, too.)

## Improvements
- **Refreshed documentation.** The website docs have been reorganized and updated to be clearer and easier to follow.
- **More breathing room on the website.** The homepage headline no longer sits too close to the top of the page.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed the supported-platform logos (Claude Code, Codex, and Copilot) that could appear broken on the website.
