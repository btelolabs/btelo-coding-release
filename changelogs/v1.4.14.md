## What's New

- On Windows, you can now switch between drives in the file browser. Tapping "up" from a drive (like C:) opens a "This PC" view listing all your disks (C:, D:, E:…), so you can finally reach files on other drives.
- On Windows, recently used Git repositories now appear in the browser. Repos on any drive are discovered, not just your home folder.
- Fixed an issue in long-running chats where sending a message could produce no response, and the only workaround was switching models. Replies now keep coming through reliably even after large messages earlier in the conversation.
