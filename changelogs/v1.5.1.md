### 2026-06-08

## What's New
- **Hear when a session is done.** The desktop app can now play a sound the moment an agent finishes its turn, so you can step away and still know the instant it's ready. Pick from a range of sounds — gentle chimes, a cat meow, and more — or turn it off entirely.

## Improvements
- **A cleaner first launch.** The opening guide now takes you straight to setup, and the welcome screen leads with what Btelo Coding does for you — manage your agents and keep them in sync across phone and computer.
- **You can always continue.** On the coding-agent setup step, you can move ahead even if nothing is installed yet.
- **Sharper website downloads.** The Mac and iOS buttons sit together on one row with the two Windows builds below, the iOS button now shows a phone icon, and the Apple and partner logos render crisply again.
- **Simpler website controls.** The header now has a one-tap light/dark toggle and a separate language picker, instead of a single combined menu.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed the supported-platform logos (Claude Code, Codex, and Copilot) that could appear broken on the website.
- Assorted polish and stability fixes.
